# plain RSS privacy policy — publication draft

Updated October 2, 2026.

## On your device

Feed URLs and titles, downloaded articles, read and starred status, and refresh
metadata are stored locally. OPML import and export read or write the
subscription files you choose.

## Feed servers and article resources

Adding or refreshing a feed sends requests to its server, which receives the
requested URL and connection information such as your IP address. The in-app
reader displays text and basic formatting with remote content blocked by
default. If you choose Load images for an article, image servers and their
redirects may receive your IP address and image URLs. This temporary image
permission resets when you leave the article, switch articles, hide images or
move the app out of the foreground. Scripts, frames, publisher styles, fonts and
media remain blocked. Images use a temporary downloader without saved cookies,
credentials or cache; the reader uses nonpersistent website storage.

## Links and sharing

Opening an article or tapped link uses the destination service. Exporting OPML
creates a copy of your feed list at the destination you choose. Feed providers,
websites and file providers apply their own policies.

## Removing data

Deleting a feed removes its saved articles, including read and starred status.
It does not erase information already held by publishers, exported OPML files or
data in your external browser. The in-app reader does not retain persistent
website cookies or cache. Deleting the app removes local app storage; backups
and external copies are separate.

## Developer services

This version contains no developer-run analytics, advertising or account
service. Device backups and services you choose, such as sharing destinations or
file providers, follow their own settings and policies.

## Apple diagnostics

We intend to use Apple-provided crash and performance information, when
available under your Apple diagnostic-sharing choices, only to troubleshoot
problems and improve reliability. App diagnostic logging should not
intentionally include document text, task contents, feed bodies, private
filenames, URLs, tokens or other private content. Apple controls its
system-generated reports; we cannot guarantee those reports contain no sensitive
information.

## Feedback on iOS and Android

Send Feedback opens a draft in your email app or the contact form in your
browser. The email draft includes the app version, the iOS or Android version
and the device model. Your feeds and articles are not included. Nothing is sent
unless you send it.

## Questions

Contact opens our website. We use the name, email address and message you
provide to respond to your request. Service providers may process this
correspondence for us. We retain it as needed to resolve the request or meet
legal obligations. Use the same contact form to request deletion.
